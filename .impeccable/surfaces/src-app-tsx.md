# Surface: home page and BellyDSP case study (full redesign)

Scope: the home page (`index.html`, `src/App.tsx` and `src/components/`) and the BellyDSP case study (`projects/bellydsp/`, `src/pages/BellyDspPage.tsx`). Replaces the Studio Rack world entirely; the old near-black plus one glowing green accent look is now an anti-reference. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport). Content, data files, résumé PDF, and PRODUCT.md rules are unchanged.

Audience and job: recruiters and engineers screening for Summer 2027 internships; they must find the résumé, the best project, and a way to reach Sean within seconds. User answers: first screen should be interactive; keep a dark theme; must not feel gimmicky or flashy, like a generic template, or AI-generated.

Chosen direction: Liner Notes (decision round, IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed 7fd1e149).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Sean's work pressed as a record: the sleeve, the vinyl, the tracklist, and liner-note credits that say exactly who played what. Refuses the hero-plus-card-grid developer portfolio and its neon-on-black skin.

OWN-WORLD: Matte blue-black sleeve board with faint grain and a ring-wear circle; off-white sleeve ink, one label red, slate fine print; a halftone duotone cover cropped from the real BellyDSP amp; a black vinyl with visible track gaps and a red center label; Archivo in two widths (expanded heavy for titles, normal for text) as the label's house type; catalog numbers, side letters, and tiny credits as the system's labels.

STORY: The visitor sees a record with Sean's name on it, hovers the tracklist and watches the tonearm find each track's groove, understands he ships real things and credits his tools honestly, then opens the résumé, a track's liner notes, or the booking (contact) line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin top bar (name and catalog number left; Side A, Side B, Liner notes, Booking, and a Résumé button right). Left: the square sleeve (about 520px) with the duotone amp cover and small set type. Middle: the vinyl pulled half out of the sleeve's right edge, a tonearm above it. Right column: "Sean Snaider" in expanded heavy type, a one-line role, the tracklist (A1 to A3 projects, B1 to B3 roles) as the working nav, and Résumé plus Email actions. Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a track swings the tonearm to that track's band on the vinyl and lights the band; clicking drops to that track's liner spread.

FORM: Liner Notes, 2000s math rock and emo LP sleeves (photo cover, tracklist, credits); position 1 of 7 on the ordered list, taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned direction (5, The Setlist); seed key 7fd1e149.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
